# Footer

The Required Footer establishes [Illinois branding](https://brand.illinois.edu/) and provides consistent university resources across websites. All color variables described in this documentation can be found within the collection of [hosted brand assets](https://web.brand.illinois.edu/) also known as a content delivery network (CDN).

## 3.1 Position of the footer
- The header touches the bottom, left, and right sides of the viewport. There should not be any page content below or to the sides of the footer. 

## 3.2 Responsiveness and breakpoints
The footer has three display modes, based on viewport width:

- Desktop: 930px wide and above
- Tablet: 600-629px wide
- Phone: up to 599px wide 

## 3.3 The top stripe
The top stripe signals the beginning of the footer, it is il-blue, 8px thick, and runs the full width of the viewport. 

## 3.4 Footer components
There are 3 rows that make up the footer. Each of these rows are divided by an 8px stripe.

- The Upper footer contains site specific information displayed in two columns. The required element being your site title link.
- The Lower footer contains a campus links section made up of 3 columns and a distinct bottom row with no column. The required elements are the primary university wordmark, links to university resources, and legal links Cookie policy, Privacy policy, Copyright, and Accessibility.

### 3.4.1 Upper Footer
The upper footer is the top row of the footer, distinguished from the lower footer by its il-storm-95 background. This section of the footer should be no shorter than 178px containing the site name and contact email at minimum.
The upper footer has 2 distinct columns, left to right:

- Site and Contact information
- Optional, Buttons, and Quick Navigation Lists

#### 3.4.1.1  Site and Contact Information
The site and contact information column is 371px wide and its height is dependent on the content within. All elements of this column begin to wrap to the next line if the elements within are longer than the column width. 

##### 3.4.1.1.1 Site name
Site information consists of the site name that matches the site name in the header, linking to the homepage. The footer site name is identical to the site name size, function, and styles as the header site name. 
Size: The site name clearly labels the site you are visiting and always links back to the homepage of the current site. It is set in 24px Source Sans 700 weight in il-blue. 
Position: The footer site name is 30px below the footer's il-blue top stripe. It is aligned with the edge of the blue box containing the Block I in the header, no less than 20px from the left edge of the viewport.

##### --- Modifications ---

On Hover: The color becomes il-Altgeld and the text is underlined.
On Focus: An il-blue 4px bottom border is added and the background becomes il-arches-90.

##### 3.4.1.1.2 Social media linked icons

Social media icons should come from the official Illinois iconography library. Each icon should be linked to its corresponding social platform directly to your unit's profiles for each. 
Size: The icons are each centered inside a 25 x 25 square. 
Position: The first icon is always left-aligned with the site name above, no less than 20px from the left edge of the viewport. Each icon after it should be placed in a row with a 25px gap apart from one another. 

##### --- Modifications ---
On Hover: The icon color becomes il-orange.

On Focus: An il-blue 4px bottom border is added and the 50px by 50px background becomes il-arches-90.

##### 3.4.1.1.3 Contact information

Size: The contact information font is set in 18px Source Sans 400 weight, at a normal line height, in il-blue. 
Position: The first contact item is always left-aligned with the site name above, no less than 30px from the left edge of the viewport. The address number and street name should be on the first line, 20px below the element above it. The applicable mail code should be broken onto the next line in the same paragraph, and the last line should contain the city, state abbreviation, and zip code.
The phone number should be on its own line following the address and before the email. The line should start with the text "Phone:" before the numbers in xxx-xxx-xxxx format, linked with the "tel:" attribute. 

The website email address should be on its own line following the phone number with the text "Email:" before the email address text, linked with the "mailto:" attribute. It is allowable to replace this line with a contact form link instead.

##### --- Modifications for linked elements ---
 
On Hover: The color becomes il-Altgeld and the text is underlined.
On Focus: An il-blue 4px bottom border is added and the background becomes il-arches-90.

##### 3.4.1.1.4 Primary unit name and other parent units

Size: These elements are set in 16px Source Sans 600 weight in il-blue. If a primary unit is named in the header, it should always be the first item in this list, and link to the primary unit's website homepage. 
Position: The primary unit and other parent units are always left-aligned with the site name above, no less than 30px from the left edge of the viewport and 30px below the element above it. Each additional parent unit should be on its own line, 16px away from the one above it.

##### --- Modifications ---
On Hover: The text color is il-Altgeld and is underlined. 

On Focus: An il-blue 4px bottom border is added and the background becomes il-arches-90.

##### 3.4.1.1.5 Partner Logos

Size: Approved partner logos may appear within a xxxPX by xxxPX size.
Position: The logos should all be placed above/below the site name in the left-hand column of the upper footer. With Xpxs of space above or below the logo. Images should not appear in any other part of the footer. 

##### --- Modifications ---
Make sure that your logos are accessible on dark mode. If they are linked to another site ensure there is accessible hover and focus states as well as accessible labels or alt text. 

#### 3.4.1.2  Buttons and Quick navigation lists
The site and contact information column is 751px wide and its height is dependent on the content within. All elements of this column begin to wrap to the next line if the elements within are longer than the column width. These elements are optional customizations. 
Tablet and Mobile Breakpoint: This column wraps below the contact information column at 342px.

##### 3.4.1.2.1 Buttons
A maximum of 4 secondary orange buttons can be used to call users to take action, similar to featured links in the header, though these don't have to reflect the header. These are short action-oriented buttons. 

Size: The width of the buttons is text dependent. They have 12px of padding on the top and bottom and 16px of padding to the left and right of the text. These buttons maintain a 2px il-orange border with a radius of 6px. The button font is set at 19px Source Sans 700 weight in white. 
Position: Buttons are place in a row 30px below the blue top stripe. They are right aligned inside the right-hand column, the trailing button maintaining no less than 30px from the left edge of the viewport. Each additional sponsor should be on its own line, 16px away from the one above it.

##### --- Modifications ---
On Hover: The text is underlined and color becomes il-orange, the background fill becomes white.

On Focus: An il-blue 4px bottom border is added and the background becomes il-arches-90

Tablet and Mobile Breakpoint: Once the viewport is not wide enough for all the buttons, they should wrap with 16px between top and right sides.

###### 3.4.1.2.2 Quick Navigation Lists
A maximum of 4 quick navigation lists can be added here. These lists should be intentionally grouped and labeled with an accessible heading, a dividing line, and then a list of links. 

Note: you are allowed to put paragraphs here if needed, but this space should be used sparingly with intention and no other media.

Size: The heading text is set at 20px Source Sans 700 weight in il-blue. The dividing line is 1px thick in il-blue, it spans the full-width of right-hand column. The link text is set at 18px Source Sans 400 weight in il-blue. 

Position: The first list should appear 30px below the element above it, aligning with the site name when there are no buttons. Below the heading is the dividing line with 18px above and below it. Underneath is the list of links, left-aligned with no additional text decorations or bullets. Between each link is 24px. If a second list group is created, it should be placed below the first group with 52px between the last list item and the next list heading. If 3 or more lists are created, they should break into a 2x2 grid on desktop, and there should be a 32px gap between these columns and 52px between rows. 

##### --- Modifications ---
On Hover (linked elements only): The text is underlined.
On Focus (linked elements only): An il-blue 4px bottom border is added and the background and becomes il-arches-90.
Tablet breakpoint: All lists stack into the right column when the viewport is between 677-343px.
Mobile breakpoint: All elements in the right column wrap beneath the left column when the viewport is below 342px.

### 3.4.2 Lower Footer
The lower footer is the bottom row of the footer, distinguished from the lower footer by its il-blue background. Signaling the beginning of the lower footer is an il-orange stripe, 8px thick, and runs the full width of the footer.

##### 3.4.2.3.1 Wordmark
The official orange and white wordmark variation should be used. 
Size: 37.5px tall by 225px wide, this element should have an accessible label reading "University of Illinois Homepage" and should always link to illinois.edu.
Position: The wordmark should always be left-aligned with the site title placed 84px below the orange stripe. 

##### --- Modifications ---
On Hover: A 2px dotted il-orange border appears.

On Focus: The element background becomes il-arches-90 and the wordmark should be replaced with the primary orange and blue version. 

##### 3.4.2.3.2 Campus headings and resource links
Headings and Links to university resources, found in the lower section, are centrally managed and should be pulled in using the official api.brand.illinois.edu/footer json file. 

Size: The heading text is set at 24px Source Sans 600 weight in white. The link text is set at 18px Source Sans 400 weight in white.
Position: The three campus headings and resource link groups are separated into 3 columns that are 367px wide with a 20px gap. The columns begin 60px below and left aligning with the wordmark above it. 

##### --- Modifications ---
On Hover (linked elements only): The color becomesiIl-Altgeld and the text is underlined.
On Focus (linked elements only): The color becomes il-blue, the text is underlined, and the background becomes il-arches-90.
Tablet breakpoint: At 677px, the columns shrink to 332px wide, making the last column wrap below the first.
Mobile breakpoint: At 342px and lower, the columns stack below each other left to right.

##### 3.4.2.3.3 Legal links
The legal links row is divided from the campus resources by a 8px thick line in il-blue-30 (#2f4668) and runs the full width of the footer. The first "link" is the cookie button opens a modal and fulfills our legal requirement to display our cookie policy on all university sites. Following this are the privacy and copyright links. There is space to add custom legal links or a login button as you need, but these custom links should follow the same style guides as the other legal links and no other plain text or elements should be included here. Accessibility should always be the last link in the list and on any page.
Size: The cookie button font is set at 14px Source Sans 400 weight in white with an il-blue-30 (#2f4668) background color. It has 12px of padding on the top and bottom with 16px of padding to the left and right of the text. It maintains a 2px white border with a radius of 6px. The link text is 16px Source Sans 400 weight in white, underlined.
##### --- Modifications ---
Button on hover: The text color becomes il-blue and the background becomes white.
Button on focus: The text color becomes il-blue and the background becomes il-arches-90.
On Hover (linked elements only): A 2px dotted border is added.
On Focus (linked elements only): The color becomes il-blue, the underlined is removed, and the background becomes il-arches-90.
Mobile and Tablet breakpoint: When the links run out of room the overflowing link(s) should wrap to a new line below.
